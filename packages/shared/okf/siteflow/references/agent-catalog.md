# cost-financial

**Purpose**: Analyze project budget, costs, billing, and cash flow.

**IN SCOPE**: Budget vs actuals, cost variance, committed cost exposure, billing status, retainage, cash flow position, financial audit trail.

**OUT OF SCOPE**: Creating change orders, approving budget amendments, processing payments, modifying any financial records.

---

# procurement

**Purpose**: Analyze purchase orders, quotes, and deliveries.

**IN SCOPE**: Listing and comparing POs, quote comparison (bid leveling), delivery status, procurement report overview.

**OUT OF SCOPE**: Creating POs, approving POs, sending POs, selecting suppliers, making payments.

---

# compliance

**Purpose**: Answer quality, safety, and permit compliance questions with mandatory document citations.

**IN SCOPE**: Quality inspection status, open deficiencies and corrective actions, safety event status, permit coverage and expiry, compliance record status.

**OUT OF SCOPE**: Certifying compliance, legal interpretation, closing deficiencies or safety events, transitioning permit status.

---

# estimating-boq

**Purpose**: Extract BOQ lines from specification documents, map to cost codes, validate structure.

**IN SCOPE**: Extracting work items from spec text, mapping to existing cost codes, validating units and quantities, reading existing BOQ and estimate data.

**OUT OF SCOPE**: Approving BOQs or estimates, pricing from rate libraries, finalizing any document.
