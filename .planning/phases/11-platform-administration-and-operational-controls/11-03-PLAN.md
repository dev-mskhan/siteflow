# Plan 11-03: Organization and account lifecycle controls

## Objective
Explicit, validated lifecycle transitions for organizations and accounts, kept separate from billing delinquency and entitlement restriction.

## Tasks
1. Transition table (for example ACTIVE <-> SUSPENDED); invalid transitions rejected.
2. Required reason code and optional case reference.
3. Row lock (`SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`) or conditional `UPDATE ... WHERE status = expected`.
4. Same transaction: audit record and outbox event.
5. After commit: revoke sessions for affected accounts; invalidate permission and org-context caches.
6. Reactivation does not revive revoked sessions and re-checks subscription and entitlement state.
7. Suspension deletes no data and cancels no subscription.

## Policy decisions (user-approved)
- Suspension is read-only: tenant data stays readable by authorized users, export is disabled, and no subscription is changed.
- Org suspension requires second-operator approval (same rule as 11-01); requester cannot approve.
- Lifecycle mutations accept an `Idempotency-Key` header and are rate-limited per operator.

## Acceptance criteria
- Concurrent transitions: exactly one succeeds (integration test).
- Session revoked immediately after suspension (integration test).
- Cache invalidated after transition (integration test).
