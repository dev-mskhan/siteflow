# Plan 11-06: Operational diagnostics and safe recovery

## Objective
Bounded, authorized views of queue, webhook, notification, storage, and health state, with recovery limited to safe, idempotent actions.

## Tasks
1. Read views: PgBoss queue depth, oldest waiting job age, retries, failures, quarantined jobs; Stripe webhook inbox states; notification failures; aggregate org storage usage; health and error/latency indicators.
2. Explicit date windows, bounded result sizes, aggregation over raw scans.
3. Logs/traces: sanitized and correlated only; no unbounded log access.
4. Recovery: only idempotent operations (webhook inbox retry via the existing durable inbox). No generic job replay.
5. Redact PII and secrets in every response.

## Acceptance criteria
- Each diagnostic query has a documented bound and recorded EXPLAIN plan.
- Webhook retry does not duplicate side effects (integration test with replayed event).
- Org users get 403 (integration test).
