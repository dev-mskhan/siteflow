# Plan 11-04: Billing and entitlement administration

## Objective
Expose and act on SaaS billing and entitlements through existing Phase 10 services. No second subscription source of truth and no second Stripe integration.

## Tasks
1. Read views: plans, trials, subscription status, invoice/payment history, payment failures, webhook/reconciliation failures.
2. Entitlement overrides only where policy allows. Each override records organization, entitlement, scope, actor, reason, created_at, expires_at/revoked_at, and an audit entry.
3. Plan limits come only from the approved shared catalog; never from client input or provider payloads.
4. Invalidate entitlement caches after each override or subscription change.
5. No Stripe secrets or payment credentials in responses.

## Acceptance criteria
- Overrides expire or revoke correctly (integration test).
- Override and subscription changes stay consistent under concurrency.
- Routes require `read:billing` / `write:billing_override`.

## Dependency
11-03 plus Phase 10 plans 10-09, 10-15, 10-18, 10-19.
