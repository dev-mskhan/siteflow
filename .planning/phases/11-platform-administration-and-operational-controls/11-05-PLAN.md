# Plan 11-05: Scoped, approval-gated support access

## Objective
Time-limited, read-only-by-default support grants with explicit scope, revocation, and second-operator approval for sensitive scopes.

## Tasks
1. Table `platform_support_grants`: operator, target organization, reason/case reference, allowed scopes, status, requested_by, approved_by, approved_at, expires_at, revoked_at.
2. Workflow: request -> approve (different operator from requester) -> active -> expired/revoked.
3. Enforce grant server-side on every protected operation; org ID comes from the grant, never from the client.
4. Sensitive scopes (ownership change, platform-role change, refunds, confidential exports) are never available to ordinary grants.
5. Audit: grant, approval, use, denial, expiry, revocation.

## Acceptance criteria
- Grant cannot expand its own scope (integration test).
- Expired or revoked grants fail immediately (integration test).
- Self-approval rejected (integration test).
- Cross-organization access without grant fails.

## Policy decisions (user-approved)
- Sensitive-scope list is the four scopes above; second-operator approval is mandatory for them, and for any scope the approver chooses to grant.
- Grant duration is capped at 24 hours; approval and expiry are checked on every request, not only by a background job.
