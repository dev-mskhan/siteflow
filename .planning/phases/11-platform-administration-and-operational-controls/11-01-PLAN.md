# Plan 11-01: Discovery baseline and platform-operator permission foundation

## Objective
Establish a platform-operator authorization boundary separate from org RBAC, deny-by-default, with a recorded baseline of tests and typecheck.

## Discovery (verified)
- No platform-operator concept exists in `packages/database/src/schema`.
- Org RBAC: `roles`, `permissions`, `role_permissions` (`org.schema.ts`), enforced by `requirePermission` (`apps/server/src/modules/rbac/permission.middleware.ts` 51-96) and `RbacService.hasPermission` (`rbac.service.ts` 62-64).

## Decisions
- Representation: platform roles and permissions in their own tables, following the existing role/permission pattern, with no link to org memberships.
- Second-operator approval for sensitive support scopes (used in 11-05).

## Tasks
1. Baseline: `pnpm --filter @siteflow/server typecheck` and targeted RBAC tests. Record results.
2. Migration: `platform_roles`, `platform_user_roles`, `platform_permissions`, `platform_role_permissions`, with unique constraints and FKs to `users`.
3. Seed platform permissions (`read:organizations`, `write:organization_lifecycle`, `read:billing`, `write:billing_override`, `read:audit`, `read:diagnostics`, `write:job_retry`, `manage:support_grants`), deny-by-default.
4. `requirePlatformPermission(key)` middleware that never consults org membership.

## Acceptance criteria
- Org owner/admin gets 403 on every platform route (integration test).
- Redis cache for platform permissions; DB is authoritative on mutation paths.
- Migration applies and rolls back on real PostgreSQL.

## Policy decisions (user-approved)
- Bootstrap: first operator is seeded by a migration from an env-provided email (no hardcoded identity); idempotent.
- Platform-role grant/revoke is sensitive: requester cannot approve their own change (NIST AC-5 / SOC 2 separation of duties); second operator approves; every change writes an audit record in the same transaction.
- Every platform mutation requires a reason code; reads are rate-limited; denied platform access is audited.
- Platform support role has no tenant project or financial data access by default.

## Threats
Org admin escalating via org role; missing-permission default; stale Redis cache after role removal.
