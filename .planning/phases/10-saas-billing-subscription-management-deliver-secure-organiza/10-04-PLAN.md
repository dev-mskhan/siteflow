## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|---|---|
| Organization user to billing API | Untrusted caller input crosses authenticated tenant and permission checks. |
| Billing service to PostgreSQL | Tenant-scoped trial state and audit changes must commit atomically. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-10-04 | Elevation of privilege | Trial routes and organization context | high | mitigate | Derive organization and actor from authenticated middleware; require billing permission and verify two-organization denial through `app.inject()`. |
| T-10-05 | Tampering | Trial provisioning transaction | high | mitigate | Enforce one-trial invariant with PostgreSQL uniqueness/locking and atomically persist trial plus audit. |
</threat_model>
