## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|---|---|
| Provider lifecycle facts to local access policy | Stripe facts must be interpreted through explicit SiteFlow transitions, not as a direct entitlement toggle. |
| Scheduled worker to Phase 10 reminder transport and delivery ledger | Only minimum tenant-qualified reminder data crosses to the approved transport; successful delivery is persisted only after transport confirmation. |
| Reminder transport configuration to provider | Protected credentials and actionable sanitized failure state stay server-side. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-10-15 | Tampering | Reminder and lifecycle workers | medium | mitigate | Use registered tenant-qualified jobs with idempotent state transitions and persisted retry-safe reminder identity; `SENT` requires a confirmed transport result. |
| T-10-33 | Information disclosure | Reminder transport and delivery records | high | mitigate | Send only approved minimum reminder content through protected provider configuration; tenant-scope persisted state and redact credentials and provider errors. |
| T-10-34 | Denial of service | Reminder retry worker | medium | mitigate | Bound due-work scans and retry attempts, persist next-attempt timing and terminal failure state, and expose actionable safe failure metrics/logs. |
</threat_model>
